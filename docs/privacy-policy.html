Kode Glass — Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 5, 2026 · Document version: 1.0

1. Who we are

This privacy policy describes how KodeLabs (“we”, “us”) handles information when you use the Kode Glass browser extension (“Extension”), distributed through the Chrome Web Store.

Privacy contact: albinisrudaku@kodelabs.com

2. Scope

This policy applies only to the Extension. It does not govern third-party websites you visit, Google’s Chrome browser or Chrome Web Store policies in general, or other companies’ services except where we explain their role below.

3. Information the Extension processes

The Extension helps you run accessibility-related diagnostics and overlays on web pages you actively use with the Extension. Depending on how you use it, it may process:

  • Web page information needed for accessibility analysis—for example the page URL, page title, and document structure and content visible to the checker (such as elements, roles, labels, headings, landmarks, and detected issues).
  • Extension UI preferences stored locally in your browser (for example theme selection for the side panel via localStorage).

The Extension requests these Chrome permissions: activeTab, sidePanel, scripting, tabs, and tts (text-to-speech when you enable narration).

4. How we use information

We use the information above solely to provide Extension functionality you request: displaying diagnostics in the side panel, highlighting elements on the page, generating reports in the UI, and optional narration. We do not use it for advertising profiling inside the Extension.

5. Transmission and third parties

No developer-hosted analytics: Based on the design of this Extension, scan results and page-derived diagnostics are processed inside Chrome and exchanged between the Extension’s own components (for example the side panel, background service worker, and content scripts). We do not transmit those scan contents to servers we operate for collection or analytics.

Platform and embedded services:

  • Chrome APIs: Chrome processes Extension messaging and permissions according to Google’s practices.
  • Text-to-speech: When you enable narration, the Extension uses Chrome’s chrome.tts API. Your device and Chrome handle synthesis and audio output.
  • Fonts: The side panel UI may request fonts from Google Fonts when the panel loads; that request is governed by Google’s terms and your network environment.
  • Documentation links: If you open external help or documentation links from scan results (for example IBM Accessibility guidance URLs shown as references), those sites apply their own privacy policies.

6. Retention

Analysis results shown in the Extension exist for your session as you use the Extension until you reset state, reload, or close the UI as applicable. Local preference storage remains until you clear extension site data or uninstall the Extension.

7. Security

We rely on Chrome’s extension security model (isolated extension contexts, permission prompts, and updates via the Chrome Web Store). You should keep Chrome updated and install the Extension only from the official Chrome Web Store listing.

8. Your choices and rights

You choose when to open the side panel and run analysis. You may clear locally stored Extension UI data through Chrome’s settings for the Extension, or uninstall the Extension to remove it and its stored side-panel data.

If applicable privacy laws give you rights (such as access or deletion) with respect to personal data we process, contact us at the privacy email above. Much of what the Extension processes exists only locally on your device and is not stored by us as a cloud database tied to your identity as part of the Extension’s core operation described here.

9. Children

The Extension is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the Extension.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the Extension’s features or legal requirements change. We will revise the effective date and document version when we do. Continued use after the effective date constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
